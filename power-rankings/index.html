Power Rankings 2026

Each week's roto and all-play results for my 10-team league, and power rankings that weight recent weeks more. How it works

Weekly power rankings: this week only. Teams are ranked 1–10 in each category on the week's stats (10 points for first, ties split), and the points are summed.

Seasonally weighted power rankings: every week so far, blended, with recent weeks counting most. Each week's category roto points (not raw stats) count 0.8 times as much as the next week's, so the ranking follows current form without dropping the early season.
